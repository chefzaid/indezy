Answers to the questions people ask most about Indezy. Each answer points to the article that covers the feature in detail.

## Who is Indezy for?

Freelancers in French tech, developers, architects, data, DevOps, project and product people, whether they work through ESNs, platforms or directly with clients.

## Which sites does the feed read?

Free-Work, Collective.work, Cherry Pick, LeHibou, Yeets, WorkDispo, Next Mondays, Mindquest, Freelance-Informatique and about ten more French boards, freelance missions only. Any other posting can be added from its link.

See [Offers](/help/offers).

## Who owns my data?

You do. Your workspace is yours alone, and you can export all of it at any time, as a file you can bring back.

## Which languages?

Indezy is in French and English, and so are its activity reports, invoices and emails.

Switch language with the globe icon in the top bar, or in **Profile → Preferences**.

## Is my data visible on the member network?

No. Joining the network is your choice, from **Profile → Network**, and only members see members. Your card shows only the fields you tick (photo, headline, skills, city, availability, languages). Your opportunities, clients, contacts, documents and invoices are never published.

Before a post or a message goes out, Indezy spots your clients', ESNs' and contacts' names in the text and offers to **Anonymize** them. An opportunity shared in a message never shows its client. See [Network](/help/network).

## How do I export my data?

Open **Profile → Account** and click **Export my data**: you download all your data as JSON. See [Account](/help/account).

## Can I import my data?

Yes, from an Indezy export. In **Profile → Account → Import data**, choose the file (50 MB at most): a preview shows what is new, already here or left out, and nothing is saved before you click **Import**. The import brings sources, clients and ESNs, contacts, seasons, opportunities with their steps, and journal notes. The profile, activity reports, offers, templates and attachments are not imported.

## How do I delete my account?

Open **Profile → Account**, click **Delete my account** and type your email to confirm. The account goes on hold at once and is hidden from the network. For 30 days, signing in offers **Restore my account**. After that, everything is deleted for good. Export your data first if you want to keep it.

## Does Indezy write to my clients or recruiters for me?

Only when you ask. **Write an email** on an opportunity prepares the message, which you open in your mail app, copy, or send with **Send from Indezy**: it then goes in your name, answers come to your own address, and it says so at its foot. Likewise, cancelling a booked call does not tell the recruiter. Indezy does send a few emails you trigger yourself: network invitations, recommendation requests, and the emails meant for you (weekly digest, booked calls, network messages).

## Does Indezy sync with my calendar or mailbox?

No. There is no sync with Gmail, Outlook, LinkedIn or a calendar. The [calendar](/help/calendar) reads your interview steps and the calls booked through your link. A network event goes into your calendar as an .ics file.

## Is there a mobile app?

No mobile app or push notifications for now. Indezy adapts to a phone screen and, on the production instance, installs as a web app.

## Can I use Indezy without a connection?

Partly. Once installed, Indezy shows what it last loaded of your workspace's main pages, kept seven days. Changes need a connection. See [Account](/help/account).

## Does Indezy use AI?

No. Offer ranking, suggestions and the skills dossier are worked out from your profile, without AI.

## How do I share my documents with an ESN?

On **Documents**, click **Share**, pick the documents and how long (7, 14 or 30 days), then **Create the link**. The ESN downloads the documents without an account, and **Revoke** closes the link at any time. See [Documents](/help/documents).

## How do I stop an email from Indezy?

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also set everything in **Profile → Notifications**, and network notifications in **Profile → Network**.

## Can I connect Indezy to my other tools?

Yes, to read from it. An API token lets a script, a spreadsheet or an automation service read your workspace, and a webhook tells your tools about events (an opportunity added, an invoice paid, a call booked…). Both are created in **Profile → Security**. See [Account](/help/account).

## How do I contact support?

Click **?** in the top bar, then **Contact support**, or open **Support**. Choose what it is about, write a subject and a description, and keep **Send the technical context** ticked so we see the page and your browser. The answer shows in **My requests** and you are told by email.
