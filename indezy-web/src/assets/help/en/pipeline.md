The pipeline follows each opportunity from first contact to signature, then the mission once won. You see it as a Kanban board on the dashboard, or as a list in **Opportunities**.

## Pipeline stages

Each opportunity sits at one of these six stages, one column each:

| Stage | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact** | First exchange with the recruiter |
| **Tests & Interviews** | Tests and interviews under way |
| **Offer** | An offer is on the table |
| **Abandoned** | Declined by the recruiter |
| **Withdrawn** | Declined by you (the consultant) |
| **Accepted** | Mission accepted |

## The Kanban board

1. Open **Dashboard**.
2. Under **Display mode**, choose **Kanban**.

![The Kanban board with its six columns, from Contact to Accepted](/assets/screens/kanban-en-light.jpg)

- **Drag a card** to another column to change its stage. Into **Abandoned** or **Withdrawn**, Indezy asks for a **Reason (optional)**, then shown on the card.
- Within a column, cards keep your own order; **Pin as favorite** (the star) puts a card at the top.
- A column's **Quick-add a card** button creates an opportunity from a role, a client and a daily rate.
- Each card counts its days without activity. After the **Inactivity period (days)** (14 by default, set in **Profile → Preferences**), it is highlighted.
- **Possible duplicate** flags two cards sharing a client and a role.

> **Tip:** tick several cards to open an action bar: **Move to**, **Source**, **Add a tag**, **Archive** or **Delete**, on up to 200 opportunities.

## The opportunities list

**Opportunities** lists all your opportunities and missions, each with its place, distance and commute from your address.

![The opportunities list with its filters and cards](/assets/help/screens/projects-en.jpg)

1. Click **New project** to create one, or **From an offer link** to start from a job ad (see [Offers](/help/offers)).
2. Filter by words, daily rate, work mode, technologies, stage, season, dates, duration, client or **Archive**. Filters stay in the page's address.
3. **Export summary** downloads the opportunities starting this year as CSV.
4. **Map** shows the opportunities on a map around your home.

## Archive an opportunity

1. On an opportunity's page, click **Archive**.
2. It leaves the board and the reminders, but keeps its stage for the statistics.
3. Find it again in **Archive**, then click **Restore** to put it back in its column.

In **Profile → Preferences**, **Archive idle opportunities every night** archives every night those the board highlights. It is on for new accounts; turn it off there if you prefer to archive by hand.

## Job-hunting seasons

A season is a job-hunting period with its own pipeline and dashboard.

1. In the dashboard's **Season** picker, choose **New season**.
2. Enter a **Season name**, a **Start**, an **End** (empty while it runs), a **Target daily rate** and an **Objective**.
3. Click **Start the season**.

Opportunities without a season created within its dates join it, and each new opportunity joins the running season. The picker narrows the board to one season or **All seasons**. **Close season** ends it; deleting one keeps its opportunities.

## The opportunity page

An opportunity's page holds everything about it. **Change pipeline stage** moves it without going back to the board.

![An opportunity's page with its recruitment steps and journal](/assets/help/screens/project-en.jpg)

- Its tech stack shows as tags, with how well they match your profile.
- **Documents and links** keeps up to 20 files (job description, contract, test brief) of 50 MB each: click **Attach a file**.
- **Copy project link** copies an address that opens for your account only.

## AI help

- On a new opportunity, **Fill in from a pasted posting (AI)** reads a posting you paste (an email, a job board page, a LinkedIn message) and fills in the role, the company, the rate, the stack, the place, remote days, the start date and the duration. Check them, then save: the client is created if you do not have it yet.
- On an opportunity's page, **Draft a message (AI)** writes an application, a follow-up, a thank-you after an interview or a polite decline, in French or English, from the opportunity and a few words on what to say. Change it as you like, then **Copy** it into your email.

Each account has a monthly AI allowance; **Profile → Preferences → AI** shows it, and takes your own Anthropic key to go beyond it. The AI only sees what you typed and the opportunity concerned.

## Recruitment steps

1. Under **Recruitment steps**, click **Add a step**.
2. Enter a **Step title**, a date and time, and notes.
3. Change its status from its pill: **To plan**, **Planned**, **Waiting for feedback**, **Validated**, **Failed** or **Canceled**.
4. Under **Preparation**, add what to prepare, or **Add suggested items**, and tick them as you go.

The **Templates** menu offers **Save these steps as a template** for an ESN, a client or **Any client or ESN**, then applies a template to another opportunity. Planned steps show in the [calendar](/help/calendar).

## Journal and emails

- **Journal** lists your notes and exchanges, newest first. Write a **New note** in Markdown, optionally from **Insert a template** (call debrief, interview debrief, negotiation summary).
- **Record an exchange** logs an exchange with a contact of the client or its ESN.
- **Write an email** picks a **Template** (follow-up, rate proposal, availability announcement, rate review at renewal) and a recipient, then prepares the subject and message. Use **Open in my mail app** or **Copy**: Indezy sends nothing itself. Manage your own templates in **Profile → Emails**.

## Once the mission is accepted

- The **Contract checklist** lists what to check before signing: payment terms, notice period, non-solicitation, intellectual property, liability and insurance, rate revision, place of work. Tick each point and note what the contract says.
- The page also shows the activity reports and purchase orders: see [Timesheets and purchase orders](/help/activity-reports).

## Non-solicitation and co-optation

On an opportunity with an ESN, fill in **Non-solicitation (months)** (1 to 36). Once that mission is won, any other opportunity for the same end client, direct or through another ESN, shows a warning and a gavel on its card until the clause runs out.

The **Co-optation** card records who referred you (**I pay a bonus to who referred me**) or whom you referred (**I receive a bonus for referring**), by name or by network @handle. Pick the bonus (**No bonus**, **Fixed amount**, **Per day worked** or **Share of the mission**) and where it stands: **Promised**, **Due** or **Paid**.

## Sources

**Sources** says where your opportunities come from: job board, social media, email, phone call or SMS, with a link, a **Popularity**, a **Usefulness** and notes. Your workspace starts with the boards the offer feed reads, plus LinkedIn. Click **New source** to add one. Sources feed the filters and the [dashboard](/help/dashboard)'s source ROI.
