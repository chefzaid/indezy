The **Network** brings Indezy's freelance members together: pass missions on, ask questions, share what you learned. Only members see members, and your card shows only what you choose.

## Join the network and set up your card

Joining is your choice: your workspace (opportunities, clients, contacts, invoices) is never published.

1. Open **Profile → Network**.
2. Choose your **Handle** (`@camille_d`: 3 to 30 lower-case letters, digits or underscores, starting with a letter).
3. Under **Your card shows**, tick what members see: **Your photo**, **Your headline**, **Your skills**, **Your city**, **Your availability**, **Your languages**, **That you work remotely**.
4. Set **Your availability**: **From my won missions and my availability date**, or by hand (**Available**, **On a mission**, **Open to offers**).
5. Click **Join the network**.

**See my page** opens your member page. In **Profile → Network** you can also write **About you**, **List my won missions on my page** (role, stack and period, never the client) and **Verify with my SIRET**: the public company registry confirms your billing SIRET is in your name, and your card gets the verified badge. **Leave the network** hides your card at once; your handle stays yours if you come back.

### Say you are open to work

On your page, **I am open to work** puts the **OPEN** badge on your photo. The **Open to work now** filter in searches finds these members. Click again to go back to the availability your missions tell.

## The feed: Following, For you, Missions

![The network feed with the post composer, the Following, For you and Missions tabs, and the member's card on the right](/assets/help/screens/network-en.jpg)

**Network** in the menu opens the feed, in three tabs:

- **Following**: your posts, those of the members you follow and those naming a tag you follow, the newest first.
- **For you**: posts of the last 30 days from the whole network, ranked by your tags, your skills, your city and reactions. **Not interested**, in a post's menu, hides it and shows you fewer like it (**Undo** brings it back).
- **Missions**: every mission members pass on, with filters (skill, city, rate, start date, remote days, **Hide those in my pipeline**). Each mission shows how it matches your profile, scored as the offers are, and **Best match first** puts the best fits on top. After them come the job board offers that match your profile best.

## Post

1. Write in **What's new?** (3,000 characters at most). Mention a member with `@handle`, a skill or a city with `#tag`.
2. Pick the kind: **Post**, **Mission to pass on**, **Available**, **Question**, **Retour d’expérience** (a lesson learned) or **Poll** (2 to 4 choices, open 1 to 7 days).
3. If you like, add up to four images (each with a description) and three documents.
4. Click **Publish**, or **Keep as draft**, or **Schedule** it for later.

Before it goes out, Indezy spots your clients', ESNs' and contacts' names in the text: **Anonymize** replaces them ("a client in Paris", "a consultancy"), **Publish anyway** keeps the text as written. A post can be edited for 15 minutes after it is published.

Under a mission another member passed on, **Add to my pipeline** creates the opportunity at Contact; you choose whether to tell its author. On a question, the author can **Mark as the answer** one comment.

> **Tip:** on an offer or a declined opportunity, **Share to the network** prepares a mission to pass on, without the client's name.

Under a post: **Like** (hover or hold for Bravo, Funny, Support, Wow), **Comment**, **Repost**, **Send** in a message, and **Save** to your **Saved** list.

## Follow members and tags

- On a member's page, click **Follow**. If their card is private, the button reads **Ask to follow**.
- On a tag's page, click **Follow the tag**: its posts join your feed.
- **Members to follow**, beside the feed, suggests members with the reason (in your contacts, same skills, same city…).

## Find members

The **Search the network** bar finds members, tags and posts. Type `@` to look for members only, `#` for tags only. **Members** opens the directory in five tabs: **All members** (by skill, city, availability, remote work), **Following**, **Followers**, **Requests received** and **Requests sent**. Searches only cover what cards show.

## Communities

The **Communities** tab gathers members around a stack, a city or a trade. Click **Join**, or **Ask to join** a private community. **Create a community** makes you its moderator; you can name co-moderators.

## Events

![The Events page with the Upcoming, My answers and Organized by me tabs](/assets/help/screens/events-en.jpg)

Open **Events** from your card, beside the feed.

1. Answer **Going** or **Interested** on a meetup or a webinar (click again to take it back).
2. On the event's page, **Add to my calendar** downloads an .ics file.
3. The day before, a notification reminds you; you are told if it is cancelled.

**Organize an event** asks for a title, a start, an address or a link to join, tags and a description.

## Messages

The **Messages** tab holds your conversations and groups. You can write freely to a member as soon as one of you follows the other: **New message**, or **Message** on their page. Otherwise **Message** sends a message request: one message, waiting for their answer. Requests you receive are under **Requests**, to **Accept** or **Decline**. **New group** gathers up to 19 members.

Enter sends, Shift+Enter starts a new line. A message can be edited or deleted for 15 minutes. **Share an opportunity** (the briefcase) sends one of your opportunities as a card, without the client; its recipient adds it to their pipeline in one click. A conversation's menu offers **Mute**, **Archive** and **Download as PDF**. **Messaging**, at the bottom right of every page, lets you answer without leaving what you are doing.

## Notifications and their settings

The **Notifications** tab tells you who followed you, reacted, commented, mentioned you or reposted. Filter by **Unread**, **Mentions**, **Comments and replies**, **Reactions and reposts** or **Follows**, then **Mark all as read**. A notification's menu deletes it or turns its kind off.

In **Profile → Network**, each kind is set to **Off**, **In the app** or **Email too** (one grouped email, at most every three hours). Emails for messages unread for an hour are set in **Profile → Notifications** (see [Account](/help/account)).

In **Profile → Notifications**, **Notifications on this device** pushes your notifications, new messages, an interview starting within the hour and, on weekday mornings, the follow-ups due to the browser you are in, even with Indezy closed, once the browser allows it. Turn it on in each browser you use; a muted conversation sends none. Below it, untick what you do not want pushed: network notifications, new messages, interviews, follow-ups or your saved searches' new offers (at most hourly).

## Daily rates in the network

Beside the feed, **Daily rates in the network** gives the median daily rate of a skill, and the range where half the rates fall, for a seniority and a region or for all of them. It comes from the won missions of the members who share their rates, each freelancer counted once per skill, and a figure shows only when five freelancers or more back it.

To share yours, turn on **Share my rates with the benchmark** in **Profile → Privacy**. Your name, your clients and your contacts are never shown, and turning it off takes your rates out at once.

**ESN scorecards**, beside the feed, names the ESNs that 5 freelancers or more dealt with. For each, it shows the median offer rate, process length, answer time and payment delay, and the usual margin, from the members' own records. Your records count unless you turn off **Count my records in the ESN scorecards** in **Profile → Privacy**. The ESN is named, never you, your clients or your contacts.

## Ideas for Indezy

![The ideas board with the Suggest an idea button and ideas sorted by support](/assets/help/screens/ideas-en.jpg)

Open **Ideas for Indezy** from your card.

1. Click **Suggest an idea** and write it in one sentence. If similar ideas exist, support them instead.
2. Click **Support this idea** on the ones you want to see happen.
3. Follow their status: **Open**, **Planned**, **In progress**, **Shipped** or **Not planned**. You are told when an idea you support moves on.

## Invite your contacts

On a contact's page, **Invite to the network** emails them an invitation from you (once per contact, 20 a day at most). You are told when they join.

## Privacy: private card, mute, block, report

- **Private card** (**Approve who follows me**, in Profile → Network): you approve each follower, and only they read your posts.
- **Mute**, from a member's page, hides their posts while you keep following them.
- **Block** ends the follows both ways and hides your page from them. They are not told.
- **Report** (a post's, comment's, message's or member's menu) sends it to the moderators with a reason. The member reported never learns who reported them.
- **Browse privately**, in the **Viewers** tab of your page, lets you open pages without being named.

**Profile → Network** lists the members you muted and blocked, to undo it.
