The **Documents** page keeps the papers ESNs ask for before and during a mission, with their expiry dates, along with your trainings. Your email templates and your skills dossier (Dossier de compétences) live in your profile and are covered here too.

## Add a compliance document

![The Documents page with the Add a document button, the Your documents list and the Trainings and certifications section](/assets/help/screens/documents-en.jpg)

1. Open **Documents** in the menu.
2. Click **Add a document**.
3. Pick the type: **URSSAF vigilance certificate**, **Company registration extract (Kbis)**, **Professional liability insurance**, **Bank details (RIB)**, **Identity document** or **Other document** (then give it a **Label**).
4. Fill in **Issued on** and, if you know it, **Expires on**.
5. Attach the file: a PDF, PNG or JPEG of 50 MB at most.

The vault holds 30 documents at most. Each document's **⋮** menu offers **Download**, **Edit** and **Delete**. A deleted document also leaves the links that share it.

## Keep an eye on expiry dates

Each document shows a status: **Valid**, **Expires soon**, **Expired** or **No expiry**.

- When you leave the expiry date empty, an URSSAF certificate expires six months after it was issued, and a Kbis extract three months after.
- Documents expired or expiring within 30 days show on the [dashboard](/help/dashboard) under **Documents to renew**.

> **Tip:** upload your new URSSAF certificate as soon as you get it: an ESN opening your link always sees the current one.

## Share a pack with an ESN through a link

Rather than sending your papers as attachments, create a link.

1. On **Documents**, click **Share**.
2. Fill in **For (ESN, recruiter)**.
3. Tick the **Documents to share**.
4. Choose how long under **Link valid for**: 7, 14 or 30 days.
5. Click **Create the link**, then **Copy the link** or **Send by email**.

The link is shown only once: copy it right away. Whoever receives it downloads the documents without an account.

The **Shared links** section lists your links, until when each is open and how many times it was opened. **Revoke** closes one at once. Up to 20 links can be open at the same time.

## Keep your training record

The **Trainings and certifications** section tracks the trainings you followed or plan, and their funding.

1. Click **Add a training**.
2. Enter the **Training**, the **Provider**, the **Starts on** and **Ends on** dates, the **Hours** and the **Cost**.
3. If you earned a **Certification**, fill in **Certified on** and **Certification expires on**.
4. For funding, choose **Funding asked to**: **FIF PL**, **AGEFICE**, **OPCO** or **Another body**.
5. Track the **Funding** status: **To request**, **Requested**, **Accepted**, **Refused** or **Paid**, with the **Request deadline** and the **Amount funded**.

Indezy does not know each fund's rules: type in the year's deadline yourself. A request still to send within 14 days of its deadline, or already past it, shows in red and joins the dashboard's reminders.

A valid certification shows on your public profile and in your skills dossier.

## Email templates and their variables

Messages you send again and again (a follow-up, a rate proposal…) are written once, in **Profile → Emails**, under **Email templates**.

1. Click **New template**.
2. Give it a **Name**, a **Subject** and a **Message**.
3. Insert variables with the buttons under **Variables**, then click **Save**.

| Variable | Replaced with |
| --- | --- |
| `{{firstName}}`, `{{lastName}}` | the contact's first and last name |
| `{{company}}` | the client's company |
| `{{role}}` | the opportunity's role |
| `{{rate}}` | the daily rate |
| `{{availability}}` | your availability date |
| `{{myName}}`, `{{myPhone}}` | your name and phone number |
| `{{privacyNotice}}` | a short notice on how the contact's details are kept |

On an opportunity, **Write an email** picks a template and a recipient, then fills in the subject and message. Use **Open in my mail app** or **Copy**, or **Send from Indezy** (see [Clients, ESNs and contacts](/help/clients-contacts)). A variable without a value stays as written and is pointed out to you. A contact who asked not to be contacted is never the recipient picked first, and is pointed out when you choose them: write to them only to answer. The follow-up, rate proposal, availability announcement and rate review at renewal are always offered. A workspace keeps 50 templates at most.

Your activity report layout is set on the client's or ESN's record instead (see [Activity reports](/help/activity-reports)).

## Download your skills dossier

The **Dossier de compétences** is an A4 PDF built from your profile.

1. Open **Profile** from your account menu.
2. At the top of the page, click **Dossier de compétences**.
3. Choose **In French, named**, **In English, named**, **In French, anonymized** or **In English, anonymized**.

It holds your name and title, your city, your contact details (named version only), your presentation, your skills with their level and years, your languages, your valid certifications, then your won missions, the latest first. In the anonymized version, your initials replace your name and each client reads "Client communiqué sur demande". Nothing in it is written by AI.

> **Tip:** fill in the description and tech stack of your won missions: they are what fills the dossier.
