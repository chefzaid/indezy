The monthly activity report (the French CRA, or timesheet) states how many days you worked on a mission each month. In Indezy you fill it in with a few clicks, the client or ESN approves it through a link without an account, and it then serves to invoice.

## Where to find your activity reports

- On the page of an **Accepted** mission, the **Activity reports (timesheets)** card shows the current month.
- The **Timesheets** menu lists every mission in progress with its reports.

Move between months with **Previous month** and **Next month**. **Saved months** lists the months already recorded, with their status.

## Fill in the month

1. Choose what you record: **Full day on site**, **Full day remote**, **Half day on site**, **Half day remote**, **Absence**, **Half day absence** or **Clear**.
2. Tap the days in the calendar.
3. Or click **Fill business days** to fill every empty business day at once, following the mission's work mode. **Clear all** starts over.
4. Optionally add a **Comment for the approver**, for example a day off agreed with the manager.
5. Click **Save**.

Weekends and French public holidays are shaded. Totals update as you type: **Days worked**, **On site**, **Remote**, **Absences**, **Business days** and **Amount excl. VAT** at the mission's daily rate.

## Send for approval

1. Once the month is complete, click **Send for approval**.
2. The month is locked and becomes **Awaiting approval**.
3. The **Approval link** is shown once. Click **Copy link**, or **Send by email** to open your mail app with a message ready to send.
4. Send it to your contact at the client or ESN.

The link stays valid 30 days. If you lost it, click **Generate a new link**: the old one stops working.

> **Tip:** to fix a month already sent, click **Reopen to edit**. The month goes back to **Draft** and the link you sent stops working: send it for approval again afterwards.

## Approval by the client or ESN

Your contact opens the link without creating an account. They see the consultant, the assignment, the client, the agency, the period, the days and your comment.

1. They enter **Your full name**, which serves as a signature.
2. They click **Approve the report**, or **Reject** with a reason.

A month gets one answer only. On your card, the status becomes **Approved** ("Approved by … on …") or **Rejected** with the reason. For a rejected month, fix it, then send it for approval again.

This is a simple electronic signature, not a qualified electronic signature.

## Download the PDF

Click **Download PDF**. The document follows the template set on the mission's ESN, else on its client:

- the document title;
- the **Layout**: **One day per line (portrait)** or **Month grid (landscape)**;
- the **PDF language**;
- the split between on site and remote;
- a footer note.

The PDF prints who answered and when. The template is set in the client's form: see [Clients, ESNs and contacts](/help/clients-contacts).

## Invoice the month

A month with days worked, unless rejected, can be invoiced once. Click **Create the invoice** on the card: Indezy drafts an invoice of the days worked × the daily rate. What comes next is in [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

## End a mission

When the mission stops, click **End the mission** on its page, enter the **End date** and the **Reason**, then **End it**. Activity reports stop with the month it ends, and the dashboard and the yearly review use that day as its last. **Resume** undoes it.

## Purchase orders

Many ESNs and clients frame a mission with a purchase order. A won mission's **Purchase orders** card follows how much of each is used, from your activity reports.

1. Click **Add a purchase order**.
2. Enter the **Order number**, the **Starts on** date, the **Ends on** date (optional), and a **Ceiling in days**, a **Ceiling in euros, before VAT**, or both.
3. Save.

Usage counts the days worked in the reports between the order's start and end, at the mission's daily rate: "18 of 20 days used", "90 % used".

- From 80 % of the ceiling, the order says it is time to ask for an amendment.
- Over 100 %, it says the order no longer covers the work.
- The [dashboard](/help/dashboard) lists running orders at 80 % or more under **Purchase orders near their ceiling**.

When the client extends the mission, open the order's menu and choose **Add an amendment**: it adds days, euros or a **New end**.

> **Tip:** without a daily rate on the mission, Indezy can only count days. Add the rate to follow a ceiling in euros too.
