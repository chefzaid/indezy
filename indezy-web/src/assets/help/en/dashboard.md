The **Dashboard** tells you what needs your attention and how your mission search is going. Around it, a few tools put figures on your missions: the year in review, the reversion rate, commute times, professional kilometres and the weekly digest.

## Read the dashboard

![The dashboard with the season picker, the Customize button, the key figures and the charts by stage and work mode](/assets/screens/dashboard-en-light.jpg)

Open **Dashboard** in the menu. It is made of six blocks:

- **Key figures**: your opportunities, the average daily rate, the estimated revenue of accepted missions (with the part still to come), active opportunities, and your target rate against the average you obtained.
- **Charts**: opportunities by stage and by work mode, and the negotiated rates.
- **This week's goal**: when your mission ends within 12 weeks (or between missions), how many opportunities to add this week, fewer while some are at the interview or offer stage, and how many you added since Monday. **Announce my availability (AI)** drafts a LinkedIn post and a network post you copy or keep as a network draft; below it, **Email my ESN contacts** sends the contacts you tick at the ESNs you worked with, that answered you or that posted missions for your profile in the feed lately, one email each, with your dossier de compétences attached, its skills and missions matching what their company posted lately put first. Contacts marked **Asked not to be contacted** are never listed; every campaign email ends with a link letting its reader object, which marks them so. `{{mission}}` names the newest mission a contact's company posted for you, and **Edit** beside a ticked contact writes them their own message (**Copy** copies it, to send on LinkedIn or the ESN's portal); **Members passing on missions for you**, below the contacts, lists the network members' missions that fit you best, to answer on the network; **Prospect** (beside it, and on an opportunity turned down as **Prospect for the next mission**) opens these emails at once. **Write each one with AI** writes every ticked contact's own message at once (20 at most), from what you know of them, for you to check with **Edit** before sending. **When** sends them now, or over a few working days (9:00 to 17:00, a daily cap); the planned ones are listed under **Waiting to go**, each with **Cancel**, and one whose contact gets in touch first is not sent. Tick **Remind those who do not answer, 5 working days later** to send one reminder, with its own message, to each contact who neither answered nor got in touch otherwise. **Your campaigns** then shows what came of each sending: emails sent, answers, opportunities opened with those companies since, and missions won; **Prospect again** ticks the contacts a campaign did not reach. The other ESNs' contacts follow, marked **no exchange yet**. The list puts first the contacts at a company through which your current mission ends within 12 weeks, and learns from your campaigns: contacts whose company gave you opportunities after one come first, then those whose company paid you best, those who left campaigns unanswered last (the likeliest 20 shown first, **Show all** for the rest), and **Prospect again** leaves unticked anyone who did not answer two of them.
- **Reminders**: interviews this week, overdue invoices (each with a button for its payment reminder), follow-ups due (with how long each company usually takes to answer, the contacts who answered "not now" on the day they gave to write back, and a button to draft the follow-up with AI when the message was about an opportunity), stages to update (an exchange you recorded that turns an opportunity down or asks for an interview offers to move it), stale opportunities, missions ending within six weeks, order renewals, purchase orders near their ceiling, **Documents to renew**, contacts without activity for six months, and **On this day**.
- **Recommended for you**: the five unseen offers that match your profile best, mixed with the missions network members pass on that fit you (not those already in your pipeline), ranked together; an offer paying above the market goes up a little, one below goes down.
- **Analytics**: trends, opportunity outcomes and refusal reasons, source ROI, conversion funnels, asked against obtained rates by year, bench time, time to signature per client or ESN, **Skills in demand** and **Prospection activity**.
- **Recent opportunities**.

The **Kanban** button at the top shows your pipeline instead; **Overview** brings the dashboard back.

> **Tip:** on a renewal, **Ask for the new rate** opens an email ready to go from the **Rate review at renewal** template, with your current rate and the market median for the mission's skill.

## Customize the blocks

1. Click **Customize**.
2. Show or hide each block, and reorder them by dragging their handle or with the arrows.
3. **Usual layout** puts the original order back.

The layout is saved with your account.

## Filter by season

The **Season** picker, under the title, narrows the dashboard to one job-hunting season or shows them all (**All seasons**). A season shows its dates, its objective, its success rate and its average rate against target. **New season** starts one.

## Trends and activity

- **Last 90 days** compares opportunities added, interviews, missions won and the rate obtained with the 90 days before. You can share this summary on the [network](/help/network), without any client or revenue.
- **Prospection activity** shows the past year, one tile per day, as GitHub does. Hover a tile to see its opportunities and interviews; click it to keep its card open and open an opportunity.
- If your profile sets **Days to bill per year**, the **Billed days** card compares your validated activity reports with that target and projects the year end at the current pace.

## Year in review

**Year in review**, in the menu, sums up one year: revenue, days worked, average rate and missions, against the year before; each mission with its days (estimated and marked with an asterisk when no activity report covers them); opportunities added, won and refused with the reasons; the skills most asked for; and the network you built. **Download PDF** exports it on one A4 page.

## Work out your reversion rate

The reversion rate (taux de réversion) is the share of the gross daily rate you keep. You set it at the top of the **Opportunities** list.

1. Open **Opportunities**.
2. In the **Reversion Rate** card, type the **Reversion rate (%)**, or your **Income tax / PAS (%)** to work it out (100% less 40% of social charges and the tax).
3. Click the save icon to keep it in your profile.

Each opportunity then shows its net daily rate and its net monthly and yearly income.

## Sort by commute time and see the map

1. In **Opportunities**, choose **Sort by** → **Commute time**.
2. Choose **Car** or **Transit**.

Missions are ordered by their monthly commute there and back at rush hour; fully remote ones come first. Without a Google Maps key on the server, the time is an **estimate** from the straight-line distance. Your address must be in your profile. An opportunity's place of work replaces the client's address.

An opportunity's page shows its **Commute** card and its effective daily rate, commute deducted. Fill in **Cost per kilometre driven** and **Monthly transport pass** in your profile to count them. **Longest drive to a mission** and **Longest public transport trip to a mission** flag the missions that are too far.

**Map**, on the opportunities list, places each opportunity on a map around your home, coloured by stage.

## Professional kilometres

On the **Timesheets** page, **Professional kilometres** downloads this year's or last year's kilometres as CSV: one row per month and mission, with the days on site of its validated activity reports and the round trip by car, then a total per client and the year's total. Give your car's fiscal horsepower (and whether it is electric) in **Profile → Preferences**, and a last line gives the amount on the official kilometre scale for the year, to claim in frais réels or hand to your accountant.

## The weekly digest

Every Monday morning, Indezy can email you: your pipeline by stage, overdue invoices, interviews of the next 7 days, follow-ups, opportunities gone quiet, this week's goal and the missions ending, the week's best offers and, if you are a member, the network's activity. It is off by default.

1. Open **Profile → Notifications**.
2. Turn on **Email me a weekly digest**.
3. Under **What the digest gives**, keep or leave out each section.

A week with nothing to say sends nothing. See also [Account](/help/account).
