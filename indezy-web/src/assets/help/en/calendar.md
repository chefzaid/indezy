The **Calendar** gathers your dated interview steps and the calls recruiters book with you. Below it, the **Booking link** lets them pick a time themselves, with no back-and-forth emails.

## Read your calendar

![The calendar in Month view, with the Month and Upcoming buttons, the arrows to change month and the Today button](/assets/help/screens/calendar-en.jpg)

1. Open **Calendar** in the menu.
2. Pick the view at the top right:
   - **Upcoming**: your scheduled interview steps and booked calls, soonest first;
   - **Month**: the month's dated steps, coloured by status, with booked calls.
3. In **Month** view, move between months with the **Previous month** and **Next month** arrows, or go back to **Today**.
4. Click a day to show its list below the grid.

Clicking a step opens its opportunity. The view you pick is kept in the page address, so you can bookmark it.

## Where the steps come from

You do not fill the calendar by hand: it reads the interview steps of your opportunities (see [Pipeline](/help/pipeline)).

- A step shows as soon as it has a date. Its status (**To plan**, **Planned**, **Waiting for feedback**, **Validated**, **Failed**, **Canceled**) sets its colour.
- The **Upcoming** view only shows **Planned** steps from now on.
- Follow-ups due and opportunities gone quiet are not in the calendar: they show in the [dashboard](/help/dashboard) reminders.

> **Tip:** Indezy does not sync with Google Calendar or Outlook. For a network event, **Add to my calendar** downloads an .ics file (see [Network](/help/network)).

## Set up your booking link

The **Booking link** card, below the calendar, gives recruiters a public page to book a call with you, without an account.

1. Click **Set up the link**.
2. In **Settings**, tick **Recruiters can book through the link**.
3. Give a **Title of the page**.
4. Choose the **Call length** (15 to 60 minutes) and the **Gap around each call** (0 to 30 minutes).
5. Set the **Minimum notice** (up to 48 hours) and **Booked up to** (7 to 60 days ahead).
6. Under **Hours you open**, give one range of hours for each day you take calls.
7. Save, then click **Copy the link** and paste it in your emails or signature.

Hours are in Paris time and French public holidays are left out. Visitors see the times in their own time zone.

## What the recruiter sees

On your booking page, the recruiter picks a free time, then gives **Your name**, **Your email** and, if they wish, their **Company** and what the call is about. They confirm with **Book this call**.

A time is free when it overlaps neither a call already booked nor a planned interview step (counted as one hour), gap included. One address can book 5 calls an hour, and the link takes 20 bookings a day at most.

## Manage booked calls

- Each booked call shows on the card, under **Calls booked**, and in the calendar.
- You get an email for each booking. You can turn it off in **Profile → Notifications**, under **Booked calls** (see [Account](/help/account)).
- **Cancel the call** removes it. Indezy does not tell the recruiter: write to them yourself.
- A webhook subscribed to the **Call booked** event receives each booking (see [Account](/help/account)).

## Change or turn off the link

- To pause bookings, untick **Recruiters can book through the link** in **Settings**. The page then says the link does not work.
- **New link** gives your page a new address: the old one stops working at once.

> **Tip:** if a link spread further than you meant, use **New link** and give the new address only to the recruiters you choose.
