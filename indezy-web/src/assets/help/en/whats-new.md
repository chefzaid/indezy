What's new in Indezy, the most recent first. Each feature has its article in the help.

## November 2026

### Tell your ESN contacts you are available

From **Announce my availability (AI)**, email the contacts you tick at the ESNs you worked with, your dossier de compétences attached. See [The dashboard](/help/dashboard).

### Screenshots for support

Add up to three screenshots or a PDF to a request to support. See [Frequently asked questions](/help/faq).

### Search the help from any page

The help panel (**?** at the top) now searches all the help, and offers **Report a problem**, **Suggest a feature** and **My requests**. See [Getting started](/help/getting-started).

### Your profile from your CV or LinkedIn

Fill in your profile from your CV with AI, or from LinkedIn's archive of your data without AI: you tick what to take, and nothing is saved until you save your profile. See [Account, security and data](/help/account).

### This week's goal

When your mission ends within 12 weeks, the dashboard sets how many opportunities to add this week and shows your progress. See [The dashboard](/help/dashboard).

### Skills in demand you lack

The **Market** panel of the offers now shows the skills the offers matching yours ask for that your profile lacks, by share of those offers. See [The offer feed](/help/offers).

### Emails sent from Indezy

Send an email to a contact from **Write an email**, in your name with answers to you, recorded in their exchanges, and know when it is opened if you ask. See [Clients, ESNs and contacts](/help/clients-contacts).

### AI help

Fill a new opportunity in from a pasted posting, and weigh an opportunity against your profile, ask the AI about it, ask whether a feed offer is worth applying to, download your dossier de compétences tailored to it, get a rate to ask on the opportunity form, polish an email before sending it, and draft an application, a follow-up, a thank-you or a decline from its page. A monthly allowance comes with each account, or use your own Anthropic key in **Profile → Preferences → AI**. See [Pipeline and opportunities](/help/pipeline).

### Daily rates in the network

Beside the feed, see the median daily rate of a skill by seniority and region, from the members who share their rates (**Profile → Privacy**), shown once five freelancers back a figure. See [The member network](/help/network).

### Reviews of clients and ESNs

On a client's page, read what members who worked with it say of it, and review it yourself once you won a mission with it; reviews that name someone or accuse wait for the moderators. See [Clients, ESNs and contacts](/help/clients-contacts).

### Expenses

**Expenses**, in the menu: record your business expenses and see what they cost you net under your status (micro-entreprise, EI, company or portage). See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Invoices in dollars, pounds and Swiss francs

Invoice an international client in its currency, at the rate to the euro you type for the issue date: the PDF, Factur-X and the accounting export state the euros too. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Late-payment interest

In **Profile → Billing**, type the late-payment rate your terms state: your invoices and quotes state it, and an overdue invoice shows the interest it is owed per day late and the €40 recovery indemnity. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Tidier by default

New accounts archive their idle opportunities every night and anonymize contacts after three years without exchange, as the CNIL advises. Both can be changed in **Profile → Preferences**; existing accounts keep their choice.

### Your privacy in one place

**Profile → Privacy** gathers all your consents, with a new setting: **Suggestions by my contacts**, to no longer be suggested to members who have your address in their contacts. See [Account, security and data](/help/account).

### The help center

**Help**, in the left menu: how Indezy works, screen by screen, with a search. The legal notice, terms of use and privacy policy are online.

### Co-optation and bonuses

On each opportunity, the **Co-optation** card notes who referred the mission to you, or whom you referred, and the bonus: fixed, per day worked or a share of the mission, counted from your timesheets. See [Pipeline and opportunities](/help/pipeline).

### The member network grows

- **Events**: meetups and webinars, with **Going** or **Interested**, a reminder the day before and adding them to your calendar.
- **Ideas for Indezy**: suggest and support the ideas you want to see in the app.
- **Message requests**: write to a member who does not follow you; they accept or decline.
- **Invite to the network** from a contact's page.
- **Missions**: the job boards' offers matching your profile come after the members' ones.
- **Not interested** on a **For you** post, and the **Open to work** badge on your card.
- **Communities** by technology or city, and the weekly digest telling your network's week.

See [The member network](/help/network).

### A clearer layout

The network's pages, **Messages** and **Members** included, share the same three columns; every notification has the same height.
