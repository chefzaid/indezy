What's new in Indezy, the most recent first. Each feature has its article in the help.

## November 2026

### Your URSSAF declarations

The Invoices page estimates your URSSAF contributions on the revenue received, and lists each month or quarter to declare with its deadline. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### The micro-entreprise and VAT franchise ceilings

The Invoices page now measures the revenue you received this year against the micro-entreprise ceiling and the VAT franchise thresholds, and says when you get close or pass them. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### A dossier focused on each company

The dossier de compétences attached to a prospection email now puts first the skills and missions matching what the contact's company posted lately. See [Dashboard](/help/dashboard).

### Network members' missions in your prospection

Below the contacts of **Email my ESN contacts**, **Members passing on missions for you** lists the network missions that fit you best, to answer their members on the network. See [Dashboard](/help/dashboard).

### An automatic reminder for prospection emails

**Email my ESN contacts** can remind, 5 working days later, the contacts who did not answer, once, with its own message. See [Dashboard](/help/dashboard).

### Each prospection email written with AI

In **Email my ESN contacts**, **Write each one with AI** writes every ticked contact their own message from yours, from their company, the missions it posted and your last exchanges. See [Dashboard](/help/dashboard).

### A live preview of your documents

In Profile → Billing, the preview now shows every page, of an invoice or of a quote, and draws itself again as you change the settings. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Style your mentions and notes

In **Order of the body**, the mentions and the notes of your invoices and quotes take their own size, alignment, font, colour, bold, frame and shading. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Header and footer zones on your documents

Your invoices and quotes can carry a footer in three zones and a header on the following pages, with placeholders such as your logo, the page count or your SIRET. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Word templates in three layouts, and a filled example

**Download a template to start from** now offers the Simple, Modern and Classic layouts, and a filled example to see the result before editing. See [Document templates](/help/document-templates).

### Deal-breakers on every offer

The feed's cards flag in red what can rule an offer out: on site every day, a rate under your target, beyond your commute, a start before you are free, portage required or a permanent job. See [The offer feed](/help/offers).

### Mark invoices paid from your bank statement

**Match my bank statement** on the Invoices page reads your bank's CSV export, matches its credits with the invoices awaiting payment, and marks those you keep paid on their day. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Your record with each ESN

A client's or ESN's page now shows your offer rate with it, how long its processes take, its margin on the client's rate and how soon it answers, from your own data only. See [Clients, ESNs and contacts](/help/clients-contacts).

### Choose the order of your invoices and quotes

**Order of the body** in Profile → Billing moves the lines, the mentions and the notes up or down, and can leave the signature box out of your quotes. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Your next prospection ranked by the last ones

In **Email my ESN contacts**, the contacts whose company gave you opportunities after a campaign come first and those who left campaigns unanswered come last; **Prospect again** leaves unticked anyone who ignored two. See [Dashboard](/help/dashboard).

### Spread your availability emails over working days

**Email my ESN contacts** can plan the emails over a few working days, office hours only, with a daily cap; one whose contact gets in touch first is not sent. See [Dashboard](/help/dashboard).

### Leave out a sector you keep hiding

When many of the offers you hide mention the same sector (banking, defence…), the feed offers to leave out the offers mentioning it. See [Freelance offers](/help/offers).

### Three layouts for invoices and quotes

Choose **Simple**, **Modern** or **Classic** in Profile → Billing. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Your letterhead under invoices and quotes

Upload a PDF letterhead in Profile → Billing: your invoices and quotes are drawn over it, and stay Factur-X files. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Your own Word template

**Word templates**, in Profile → Billing, fill a layout you made in Word with each invoice or quote, for **Download as Word**. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Your own font on invoices and quotes

**Upload my font**, in Profile → Billing, prints your documents in a TrueType font of your choice, when its licence allows embedding. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Invoices and quotes in Word

**Download as Word** gives an invoice or a quote as a .docx laid out like its PDF. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Your CV in your profile

**My CV**, under your personal information, keeps your CV to download and attach to applications; the profile import can keep the CV it reads. See [Account, security and data](/help/account).

### Past positions and education

The profile keeps the positions you held before Indezy and your schools, filled from your CV or LinkedIn if you like; the dossier de compétences prints them after your won missions, and your public profile shows the ones you choose. See [Getting started](/help/getting-started).

### Your LinkedIn profile's PDF

**Import my profile** also reads the PDF of LinkedIn's **Save to PDF**, without AI and without waiting for LinkedIn's archive. See [Account, security and data](/help/account).

### Certifications from your CV or LinkedIn

**Import my profile** now finds your certifications in your CV or LinkedIn's archive and adds the ones you tick to your trainings, with their dates. See [Account, security and data](/help/account).

### The quarterly market report

The daily rates the feed's missions stated each quarter, by skill, seniority and region, open to anyone at **/market-report**. See [The offer feed](/help/offers).

### Tell your ESN contacts you are available

From **Announce my availability (AI)**, email the contacts you tick at the ESNs you worked with, your dossier de compétences attached. See [The dashboard](/help/dashboard).

### Screenshots for support

Add up to three screenshots or a PDF to a request to support. See [Frequently asked questions](/help/faq).

### Search the help from any page

The help panel (**?** at the top) now searches all the help, and offers **Report a problem**, **Suggest a feature** and **My requests**. See [Getting started](/help/getting-started).

### Your profile from your CV or LinkedIn

Fill in your profile from your CV with AI, or from LinkedIn's archive of your data without AI: you tick what to take, and nothing is saved until you save your profile. See [Account, security and data](/help/account).

### This week's goal

When your mission ends within 12 weeks, the dashboard sets how many opportunities to add this week and shows your progress. See [The dashboard](/help/dashboard).

### Skills in demand you lack

The **Market** panel of the offers now shows the skills the offers matching yours ask for that your profile lacks, by share of those offers. See [The offer feed](/help/offers).

### Emails sent from Indezy

Send an email to a contact from **Write an email**, in your name with answers to you, recorded in their exchanges, and know when it is opened if you ask. See [Clients, ESNs and contacts](/help/clients-contacts).

### AI help

Fill a new opportunity in from a pasted posting, and weigh an opportunity against your profile, ask the AI about it, ask whether a feed offer is worth applying to, download your dossier de compétences tailored to it, get a rate to ask on the opportunity form, polish an email before sending it, and draft an application, a follow-up, a thank-you or a decline from its page. A monthly allowance comes with each account, or use your own Anthropic key in **Profile → Preferences → AI**. See [Pipeline and opportunities](/help/pipeline).

### Daily rates in the network

Beside the feed, see the median daily rate of a skill by seniority and region, from the members who share their rates (**Profile → Privacy**), shown once five freelancers back a figure. See [The member network](/help/network).

### Reviews of clients and ESNs

On a client's page, read what members who worked with it say of it, and review it yourself once you won a mission with it; reviews that name someone or accuse wait for the moderators. See [Clients, ESNs and contacts](/help/clients-contacts).

### Expenses

**Expenses**, in the menu: record your business expenses and see what they cost you net under your status (micro-entreprise, EI, company or portage). See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Invoices in dollars, pounds and Swiss francs

Invoice an international client in its currency, at the rate to the euro you type for the issue date: the PDF, Factur-X and the accounting export state the euros too. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Late-payment interest

In **Profile → Billing**, type the late-payment rate your terms state: your invoices and quotes state it, and an overdue invoice shows the interest it is owed per day late and the €40 recovery indemnity. See [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

### Tidier by default

New accounts archive their idle opportunities every night and anonymize contacts after three years without exchange, as the CNIL advises. Both can be changed in **Profile → Preferences**; existing accounts keep their choice.

### Your privacy in one place

**Profile → Privacy** gathers all your consents, with a new setting: **Suggestions by my contacts**, to no longer be suggested to members who have your address in their contacts. See [Account, security and data](/help/account).

### The help center

**Help**, in the left menu: how Indezy works, screen by screen, with a search. The legal notice, terms of use and privacy policy are online.

### Co-optation and bonuses

On each opportunity, the **Co-optation** card notes who referred the mission to you, or whom you referred, and the bonus: fixed, per day worked or a share of the mission, counted from your timesheets. See [Pipeline and opportunities](/help/pipeline).

### The member network grows

- **Events**: meetups and webinars, with **Going** or **Interested**, a reminder the day before and adding them to your calendar.
- **Ideas for Indezy**: suggest and support the ideas you want to see in the app.
- **Message requests**: write to a member who does not follow you; they accept or decline.
- **Invite to the network** from a contact's page.
- **Missions**: the job boards' offers matching your profile come after the members' ones.
- **Not interested** on a **For you** post, and the **Open to work** badge on your card.
- **Communities** by technology or city, and the weekly digest telling your network's week.

See [The member network](/help/network).

### A clearer layout

The network's pages, **Messages** and **Members** included, share the same three columns; every notification has the same height.
