Indezy brings your mission search and the missions themselves into one place: offers, pipeline, clients, timesheets and invoices. This guide helps you set up your workspace and fill in the profile everything else relies on.


On any page, the **?** button in the top bar opens the help of that page beside it. Search all the help from there, report a problem, suggest a feature or read your requests to support. A small **?** beside a setting opens the help at what explains it.

## Your account

1. On the home page, click **Sign in**.
2. Depending on your instance, enter your email and password, or choose **Continue with SSO**.
3. If two-factor authentication is on, enter the **6-digit code** from your authenticator app.

Open sign-up is not available on every instance yet: if you see no **Create my account** button, accounts cannot be created there. A password has at least 8 characters.

> **Tip:** there is no password reset for local accounts yet. Keep yours in a password manager. Account security is covered in [Account, security and data](/help/account).

## The welcome steps

The first time you sign in, Indezy opens **Welcome to Indezy**. Three short steps set up your workspace, and you can change all of it later in your profile.

1. **About you**: your first and last name, your **Title**, up to 30 **Skills** (type one, then Enter) and your **Address**, used to work out commute times. **Start from LinkedIn or my CV** fills them in from your CV or LinkedIn's archive of your data, after you tick what to take.
2. **Rate and availability**: your **Target daily rate** and the date you are **Available from**.
3. **Recruitment process**: pick **Through an ESN** or **Direct client** to save the usual interview steps as a template, or **Not now**.
4. **Ready**: choose where to start, **Add an opportunity**, **Browse freelance missions** or **Open the dashboard**.

You can also click **Skip the setup** at any time.

## Fill in your profile

Open **Profile** from the account menu. The **Personal information** tab holds the identity the whole app uses.

![The Profile page, Personal information tab, with skills, target daily rate and availability](/assets/help/screens/profile-en.jpg)

1. Enter your name, contact details, address and city, company and title.
2. Add a presentation, your website, LinkedIn, GitHub, a photo and your **Languages**.
3. In **Skills**, type a skill and press Enter, or pick a suggestion. Optionally set a **Level** (from **Beginner** to **Expert**) and **Years**.
4. Enter your **Target daily rate** (a whole number of euros) and the date you are **Available from**.
5. Save.

These fields do real work:

- your city and address give the distance and commute to every offer and mission;
- your skills, target rate, city and availability score the [offers](/help/offers) against your profile;
- the target rate also drives suggested searches, the dashboard and new seasons.

A profile names at most 50 skills. Different spellings count as one skill ("Spring Boot" and "spring-boot"): Indezy points it out and offers to merge duplicates with **Use …**.

> **Tip:** the optional **Days to bill per year**, **Longest drive to a mission** and **Longest public transport trip to a mission** fields add a billed-days card to the [dashboard](/help/dashboard) and flag missions too far from home.

## Past positions and education

Under your personal information, **Past positions** and **Education** hold what you did before or outside Indezy.

1. Click **Add a position**: the title, the client or employer, the months it started and ended (leave the end empty while it runs), what you did and the technical environment.
2. Click **Add a school**: the school, the degree and its years.
3. Tick **Show on my public profile** for those your public profile (below) should show.

They are not opportunities: your revenue, seasons and dashboard leave them out.

## Download your skills dossier

The **Dossier de compétences** button on the Profile page builds an A4 PDF of your profile: presentation, skills, languages, valid certifications, won missions, then your past positions and education.

1. Click **Dossier de compétences**.
2. Choose **In French, named**, **In English, named**, **In French, anonymized** or **In English, anonymized**.

The anonymized version leaves out your contact details and shows clients by their initials.

## Import data

To fill in your profile from your CV or LinkedIn's archive, see [Fill in your profile from your CV or LinkedIn](/help/account). You can also bring in an Indezy export, for example from another account.

1. Open **Profile → Account → Import data**.
2. Click **Choose an export file** (50 MB at most).
3. Read the preview: each record is marked new, already here or left out, with the reason.
4. Click **Import**. Nothing is saved before.

The import brings sources, clients and ESNs, contacts, seasons, opportunities with their steps, and journal notes. It does not bring the profile, activity reports or attachments. For your LinkedIn contacts, see contact import in [Clients, ESNs and contacts](/help/clients-contacts).

## Your billing identity

Before you issue an invoice or send a quote, complete **Profile → Billing**: legal name and form, SIRET, VAT or VAT exemption, billing address, IBAN and payment term. Details are in [Quotes and invoices](/help/invoices-quotes).

## Your public profile

A public page shows your profile to anyone with the link: name, title, city, presentation, skills, languages, links, photo and availability. Your email, phone, address and workspace stay private.

1. Open **Profile → Public profile**.
2. Turn on **Show my public profile**.
3. Choose an **Address**: 3 to 40 lower-case letters, digits and dashes.
4. Click **Copy the link** to share it.

While the profile is hidden, its link shows nothing.

## Testimonials

In **Profile → Public profile**, the **Testimonials** section lets you ask a client for a few words.

1. In **Ask**, pick a contact, or **External person (outside your contacts)** and enter their first name, last name and email.
2. Click **Ask for a testimonial**.
3. Indezy emails a personal link when it knows an address. The link is also shown once: copy it with **Copy the link** to send it yourself.
4. The person writes the testimonial without an account. The link stays open 30 days.
5. A received testimonial is **To review**. Click **Show on my public page** to publish it, or **Hide** to take it down.

Indezy emails at most 20 requests a day.
