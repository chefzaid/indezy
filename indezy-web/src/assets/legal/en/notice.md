## Reporting illegal content

The Service lets its members publish content (posts, comments, messages, files, events). For that content, the publisher acts as a hosting provider within the meaning of the LCEN and of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 on digital services (DSA).

Anyone may report content they consider illegal:

- in the Service, with the **Report** function of a post, a comment, a message or a member's page;
- or by email to contact@swirlit.dev.

To be handled quickly, a report by email states:

- why the content is considered illegal;
- the exact address of the content (its link) or anything that makes it possible to find it;
- your name and email address;
- a statement confirming that you act in good faith and that the information given is accurate and complete.

An abusive report, presenting as illegal content known to be lawful in order to have it removed, may make its author liable.
